## Material from other providers

These items keep the terms of their providers.

- `data/map_panels/` and the before/after figure contain modified Copernicus Sentinel
  data (2017, 2026), used under the Copernicus Sentinel data terms.
- `data/sea_level/` holds the Caspian altimetry series of NASA's Global Water Monitor.
- Names and designations in the protected-areas tables come from the World Database on
  Protected Areas (UNEP-WCMC and IUCN). The database itself is not redistributed here.
- The tables derived from JRC Global Surface Water, GHS-POP, GEBCO, HydroLAKES and
  Dynamic World are summary statistics. The source datasets are not redistributed here.
